About

I started in writing and editing, spent nearly a decade in mixed-methods health services research at UW–Madison beginning in 2009,I keep a small grid that plots this arc, professional and personal, against the weeks of a single life: life in weeks. and moved into data engineering when I realized the tools I needed to study complex healthcare systems didn't exist yet.

The methodological thread runs from grounded theoryGrounded theory, developed by Glaser and Strauss in 1967, builds theory inductively from coded qualitative data rather than testing hypotheses against it. In practice this means reading transcripts, labeling passages, comparing labels across transcripts, and letting structure emerge. in qualitative research, through logistic and linear regression in population health, through time series analysis in Stars forecasting. Different instruments, the same underlying question about how complex systems behave under measurement. Right now that means Medicare Star Ratings, HEDIS (Healthcare Effectiveness Data and Information Set) pipelines, and data integration across claims and eligibility sources.Current role: Manager of Data Science & Engineering at Baltimore Health Analytics, Madison WI.
